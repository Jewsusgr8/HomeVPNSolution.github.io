01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

From experiment to reliable infrastructure

The original goal was simple: route my devices' traffic through my home internet connection while traveling. The first solution worked... but it depended on a laptop I also wanted to take on vacation to play WoW Forever. That constraint drove a redesign around dedicated hardware.

⌁

Encrypted tunneling

WireGuard provides an encrypted connection between a GL.iNet travel router and a VPN endpoint at home.

◇

Network engineering

Routing, NAT, firewall configuration, dynamic DNS, and remote troubleshooting are central to the project.

↻

Improving availability

The next phase moves the VPN endpoint from a general-purpose Kali Linux laptop to a dedicated Brume 2 gateway.
